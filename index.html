Spec smart

Foam backer board vs. cement backer board

Both have a place on the truck. Here's the honest, side-by-side so you can spec the right substrate for the assembly — and the schedule.

Property StreamlineEssenti foam board Cement backer board
Built-in waterproofing Integral moisture barrier Needs separate membrane
Board weight (per sheet) Lightweight — a one-person carry Heavy — typically a two-person lift
Cutting & handling Score-and-snap with a utility knife Carbide blade, silica dust, scoring tools
Mold & rot resistance No organic food source Resistant, but joints still need treatment
Install speed Faster — fewer layers, lighter handling Slower — membrane is a separate step
Best fit Showers, steam, wet floors, fast turnarounds Floors, countertops, high-impact dry areas

Need both on the same job? We stock cementitious backer too — talk to us about a mixed pallet.

Q&A

Answers to common questions

Do I need a trade account to buy from you?
We're a B2B wholesale supplier built around licensed contractors, builders, and distributors. Trade accounts unlock our best pricing and terms — but serious DIY remodelers are welcome to request a quote on any material. Either way, pricing is account-gated, so start with a quote request.
What area do you deliver to?
We stock and ship from Portland and deliver across the Pacific Northwest, with a standard 24–48 hour window on local orders. Will-call pickup is available at our PDX location. Larger or recurring jobs can be scheduled on a standing delivery cadence.
Is Essenti foam board really code-compliant for showers?
Yes. Essenti Board carries an integral moisture barrier and is engineered for code-aligned wet-area assemblies when installed per spec — bonded seams, sealed fasteners, and corners treated. We'll provide submittal documentation on request so your assembly clears inspection.
Can you size shower pans and niches to my opening?
Absolutely. We stock common sizes for fast turnarounds and build custom pans, curbs, and niches to your rough opening. Send dimensions with your quote request and we'll confirm lead time — most custom pieces ship inside the same week.

Ready to price your next job?

Send us your material list or a set of plans. We'll come back with wholesale pricing, lead times, and a delivery window — usually same day.
