Trade supply, the right way

We supply the materials that keep wet-area jobs on schedule

Streamline Essentials is a Portland wholesale supplier built around one idea: contractors deserve code-credible waterproofing and tile substrates, in stock, at trade pricing — without the showroom theater.

Our story

Started by people who've stood on the job site

We got tired of watching good crews lose hours to back-ordered membrane, two-person backer board, and big-box markups that quietly ate the margin on every wet-area bid. So we built the supplier we wished we had.

Today, Streamline Essentials stocks a focused line of waterproofing and substrate materials — including our own Essenti foam backer board — and ships it across the Pacific Northwest from Portland. No catalog bloat, no retail runaround. Just the materials a tile assembly actually needs, priced for the trade.

What we stand on

Four commitments we don't compromise

Code first

Every wet-area material we carry is spec'd for assemblies that clear inspection. We'd rather lose a sale than ship something that won't pass.

Stock you can plan around

Depth on the core line card means your schedule isn't hostage to a vendor's back-order. Predictable inventory is a feature, not a promise.

Margin-protective pricing

Wholesale terms built for trade volume. We price so the crew that does the work keeps the value — not the showroom in the middle.

Spec-literate help

Talk substrates, lippage, decoupling, and submittal docs with people who speak the language — and who pick up the phone.

How it works
