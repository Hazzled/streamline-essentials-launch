Spec smart

Foam backer board vs. cement backer board

Both earn a place on the truck. Here's the honest side-by-side so you spec the right substrate for the assembly — and the schedule.

Property StreamlineEssenti foam board Cement backer board
Built-in waterproofingIntegral moisture barrierNeeds separate membrane
Board weight (per sheet)Lightweight — a one-person carryHeavy — typically a two-person lift
Cutting & handlingScore-and-snap with a utility knifeCarbide blade, silica dust, scoring tools
Mold & rot resistanceNo organic food sourceResistant, but joints still need treatment
Install speedFaster — fewer layers, lighter handlingSlower — membrane is a separate step
Best fitShowers, steam, wet floors, fast turnaroundsFloors, countertops, high-impact dry areas
